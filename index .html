# About Nazir

Senior WordPress Developer with 6+ years of experience designing, developing, optimizing, and maintaining high-performance WordPress websites. I specialize in Elementor Pro, WooCommerce, PHP, and technical SEO — the full path from a first pixel in the page builder to a site that's fast, secure, and ranks.
